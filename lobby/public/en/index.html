Before you head out

What can I use today?

Install game environments, manage mods and shaders, download Modrinth modpacks, import supported complete packs, migrate saves and manage local servers. A local player mode is available. Microsoft sign-in has not completed Minecraft API integration and cannot yet be used to join servers requiring authenticated accounts.

Does every modpack work?

Currently supported: Modrinth online installs, .mrpack files and complete MCBBS ZIP exports from HMCL / PCL. CurseForge is not connected. Manifests that still require downloads from that service, arbitrary zipped launcher folders and packs with custom launch components are not supported.

Where do my worlds and account details go?

Games, mods and worlds stay on your computer. The Microsoft sign-in flow uses the system credential vault for tokens; the room service does not receive those tokens. Multiplayer involves room and connection data. Read our privacy notice .

Is this an official Minecraft launcher?

No. Blocklink is an independent third-party project, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Mojang or Microsoft. Minecraft and related trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Where are the source code and support channels?

Blocklink is open source under the MIT license. Explore the source code, browse releases, or report a problem. Include your app version, OS and reproduction steps. Remove account tokens and private invitations from logs.
